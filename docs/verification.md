### Opponent 1200 investigation

No option-conversion defect was found: the shipped Fairy engine receives `UCI_LimitStrength=true` and `UCI_Elo=1200`. Its upstream formula maps this target to Skill approximately −1 and fixes its handicapped choice at depth 1; increasing search time alone does not remove that handicap. This behavior was checked against the packaged pinned source and six real-worker probes at 350/1,000 ms. See [Fairy search.cpp](https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish/blob/master/src/search.cpp) and [Stockfish search.h](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/blob/master/src/search.h).

A small comparative probe evaluated 64 selected moves across eight positions including both colors, with two repetitions per configuration. Fairy1200 averaged 2.4 percentage points of win-probability loss; Stockfish19 Skill0 averaged 10.3; its minimum UCI1320 averaged 12.9; Skill4 averaged 0.2. These samples do not establish human Elo, mobile performance or monotonic difficulty. Switching 1200 to minimum Stockfish19 was not supported by these results; relabeling a stronger Skill4 as 1200 would be misleading. The configured 1200 target is retained, and human-strength calibration remains open. Temporary probe: `/private/tmp/chess-strength-probe.mjs`.

Physical iPhone Safari/PWA, nonzero safe areas, touch, enlarged text and VoiceOver remain unverified. The owner's existing reviews were not reloaded.
