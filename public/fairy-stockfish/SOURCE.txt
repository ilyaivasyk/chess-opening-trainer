These nine publisher files are redistributed without modification. The complete
fairy-source.tar.gz archive contains 112 files. All 112 file contents were compared
with the official GitHub source archive at the pinned revision and matched exactly;
no missing, additional or differing files were found. single-worker.patch changes
thread.cpp, tt.cpp, uci.cpp and ffishjs.cpp; search, evaluation and strength-option
source files are unchanged. browser_main.cpp and Makefile.browser supply the
single-worker port. They add a cfour variant definition but retain default chess.
The port disables NNUE, uses one synchronous Worker and requires no SharedArrayBuffer,
COOP/COEP headers, SIMD or external network model. The published rebuild toolchain
is Emscripten 3.1.74. A reproducible rebuild of the delivered JS/WASM has not been
performed locally; source comparison does not prove compiled-binary equivalence.
The shipped worker bridge, JS glue and WASM passed a browser-like Node VM probe
with SharedArrayBuffer and Atomics unavailable. It advertised the source's exact
strength ranges and returned legal moves at 600, 800, 1000 and 1200. This is not a
physical iPhone Safari/PWA test.

Rebuild instructions and publisher details are in SOURCE.md. Deliver the archive,
patch, browser_main.cpp, Makefile.browser, AUTHORS and COPYING.txt with these
binaries. Our worker.js is the readable UCI transport; bounded synchronous searches
are cancelled by terminating the Worker, because stop cannot interrupt a running
single-worker WASM call.

The official source declares Skill Level -20..20 and UCI_Elo 500..2850. These are
engine-calibrated targets, not verified human/FIDE/Chess.com ratings on this device.
Stockfish 19 remains the separate review engine. App-wide GPL compatibility and
App Store source/distribution obligations remain unresolved.
